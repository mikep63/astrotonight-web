Calculations

Everything is computed in your browser, nothing is fetched from a server. Object positions, transit times, and altitude curves use standard positional-astronomy algorithms (after Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms), ported from the iOS app. Sun, Moon, and twilight times come from astronomy-engine's ephemeris rather than the app's own routine, so a given time may differ from the app's by a few seconds.

Privacy

Your browser asks for your location, and it is used only within your browser, to work out what is above your horizon tonight. It is never transmitted, stored remotely, or shared. This site has no server component, no accounts, and no analytics.
